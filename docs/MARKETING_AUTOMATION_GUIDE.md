## 📅 Daily & Weekly Automated Marketing Operations

### 1. Daily Inbound & Customer Review Loop (`emails`, `sms`, `revops`)
- **Daily 8:30 AM**: Monitor admin CRM for completed jobs from yesterday.
- **Trigger**: Send automated SMS + Email asking: *"How did your clean go? Rate us in 1 click"*, directing happy clients straight to Google Business Profile for 5-star reviews.

### 2. Daily B2B Commercial Prospecting (`cold-email`, `prospecting`)
- **Target**: 25 Bay Area Tech Office Experience Managers & Medical Clinic Directors daily.
- **Workflow**: Generate personalized 3-step cold email sequences highlighting 24/7 responsiveness, W2 background-checked staff, and complimentary 15-minute facility audits.

### 3. Weekly SEO & Content Flywheel (`ai-seo`, `content-strategy`, `social`)
- **Tuesday / Thursday**: Publish and syndicate localized case studies and cleaning safety guides to rank in Google Maps, Search, and AI Answer Engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini).

### 4. Client Retention & Referral Loops (`referrals`, `churn-prevention`)
- **Monthly**: Dispatch $150 vendor referral credits to office managers who refer neighboring commercial tenants or sister offices.
