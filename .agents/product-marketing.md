## 4. Problems & Pain Points We Solve

1. **The "Disappearing Cleaner" Syndrome**: Cleaning crews do a great job in month 1, but quality steadily declines by month 3 because of high turnover and lack of management supervision.
   - *Dozeles Solution*: Regular supervisor quality audits with graded checklists and digital photo verification sent to the client.
2. **Security & Liability Anxiety**: Unvetted 1099 subcontractors entering sensitive offices, tech labs, or homes after hours.
   - *Dozeles Solution*: 100% W2 employees, comprehensive background checks, drug testing, $2,000,000 general liability policy, and bonding.
3. **Slow, Opaque Quoting**: Waiting 3-5 days for a janitorial quote while someone walks through with a pen and notepad.
   - *Dozeles Solution*: Instant online transparent pricing engine + same-day on-site or video walkthrough proposals generated within 2 hours.
4. **Toxic Chemical Smells**: Harsh bleaching agents triggering asthma or chemical sensitivities in modern offices.
   - *Dozeles Solution*: Eco-friendly, Green Seal certified, hypoallergenic, EPA List N disinfectants and HEPA filtered vacuums.

## 7. Flagship Lead Generation Offer: The Free Facility Cleanliness Score

- **Offer Headline**: *"Let Us Check How Your Existing Janitorial Contractor Is Doing — Get a Free Cleanliness Score on Their Work."*
- **The Angle**: B2B facilities rarely switch cleaning companies unprompted due to inertia. By framing the offer as an **objective quality & safety audit of their existing contractor** rather than a sales pitch, conversion rates multiply 4x–6x.
- **The 4 Audit Score Dimensions**:
  1. 🔬 **Surface ATP & Touchpoint Residue**: Swab test on high-traffic desks, keypad locks, conference tables, and breakrooms.
  2. 🧼 **Restroom & Fixture Hygiene Index**: Grout bacteria inspection, fixture disinfection, and ventilation check.
  3. 🛡️ **Cal/OSHA & Safety Compliance**: Verification of chemical safety, SDS binders, and slip-and-fall hazards.
  4. 💰 **Rate & Scope Benchmarking**: Unbiased breakdown of what they pay per sq ft vs. actual contract scope delivery.
- **Deliverable**: A 2-page executive PDF **Cleanliness & Compliance Report Card** with a letter grade (A+ through F) and recommended action items.
